Calendar Meeting Status

Know which meetings are unconfirmed, confirmed, done or cancelled - and why.

The problem

Odoo's calendar shows whether attendees accepted an invitation, but not what became of the meeting itself. Cancelled meetings stay in the calendar like any other, and nobody can tell afterwards why a meeting did not happen.

What you get

Four statuses

Unconfirmed, Confirmed, Done and Cancelled, shown in a status bar on the meeting.

Cancellation reason

Cancelling asks for a reason. It is stored on the meeting and written to the chatter.

Colored badges

The meeting list shows the status as a colored badge.

Filter and group

Find all open or cancelled meetings with ready-made filters and a group-by.

History

Every status change is tracked in the chatter.

Reversible

A meeting can be reopened or set back to Unconfirmed at any time.

How to use it

1
Open Calendar and open any meeting (or create one). A status bar with buttons appears above the form.
2
A new meeting is Unconfirmed. Press Confirm when it is going ahead.
3
After the meeting press Set as Done.
4
To cancel, press Cancel, type the reason and press Cancel Meeting. The reason is shown on the meeting.
5
Press Set to Unconfirmed (or Confirm) to bring a cancelled meeting back.
6
In the list view of meetings use the filters Unconfirmed / Confirmed / Done / Cancelled or group by Meeting Status.

Good to know

  • Existing meetings become Unconfirmed when the app is installed.
  • The status is separate from the attendees' answers (accepted / declined).
  • Cancelling does not delete or archive the meeting and does not notify attendees.
  • For a recurring event, each occurrence has its own status.
